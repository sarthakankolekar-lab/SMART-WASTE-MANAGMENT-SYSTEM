How PuneWaste Works

We leverage IoT sensors and AI-driven logistics to transform traditional waste management into a highly efficient, sustainable operation.

sensors

1. Smart Data Collection

IoT sensors placed in waste bins across Pune district monitor fill levels in real-time, instantly alerting our centralized system when a bin approaches capacity.

route

2. AI Route Optimization

Our advanced algorithm continuously analyzes traffic patterns and bin fill levels to generate the most fuel-efficient collection routes for municipal trucks.

recycling

3. Successful Disposal

Waste is collected on time, preventing overflow, and efficiently transported to recycling centers and certified landfills using sustainable practices.
